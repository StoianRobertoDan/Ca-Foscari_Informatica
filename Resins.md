- **Tensile Strength**: The highest amount of stress before failure. Typically this means that material with a higher Tensile strength can withstand more force before breaking.
- **Elongation at break:** The amount the material is stretched as a percentage of the original length. Measured by the total amount of strain before failure.
- **Young modulus:** A measure of the stiffness of a material, a higher modulus means a more stiff material. The young modulus is measured by measuring the relation between stress and strain(slope) in the linear part of the curve.
- **Flexural Strength**: Highest amount of force the material can withstand before failure.
- **Flexural Modulus**: A measure of material stiffness, considered as a ration between applied force and deformation in the elastic region.
