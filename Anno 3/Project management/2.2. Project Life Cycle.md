Project life cycle:
- Predictive life cycle (Plan-driven):
	- Plan is set up at the beginning and in case of changes, the plan must be revisited, modified and accepted.
		- Less flexible to changes. Good for:
			- Well-understood requirements.
			- Limited changes during design.
			- Business with stable requirements.
- Adaptive life cycle (Change-driven):
	- Work is divided in manageable iterations (or sprints) which scope is define and approved before the start of the iteration.
		- More flexible to changes. Good for:
			- Requirements are unclear or likely to change.
			- Innovation or experimentation is needed.
			- Active participation of the stakeholders is required.
		- Each iteration must produce something that is ready for release at the end of the time period.
