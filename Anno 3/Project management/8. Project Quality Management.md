Measurements:
- Construct vs Measure:
	- Construct (conceptual):
		- Broad concept of a topic.
		- Abstract.
		- Not directly observable.
		- May be complex.
	- Measure (Operational construct):
		- Precise definition.
		- Clear measurement procedure.
	- Examples:
		- Wealth vs GDP.
		- Intelligence vs WAIS and other intelligence test.
		- ...
- Types of measure:
	- Base measure (collected directly by interacting with the entity under measurement):
		- Length.
		- Duration.
		- Number of defects.
		- Effort.
		- ...
	- Derived measure (computed on the basis of other measures)
		- Productivity.
		- Defect detention efficiency.
		- ...
	- Objective.
	- Subjective.

	- Product Quality:
		- Functional suitability:
			- Capability of a product to provide functions that meet stated and implied needs of intended users when it is used under specified conditions.
				- Completeness.
				- Correctness.
				- Appropriateness.
		- Compatibility:
			- Capability of a product or its component to exchange information with other products, systems or components, and/or perform its required functions, while sharing the same hardware or software environment.
				- Co-existence.
				- Interoperability.
		- Reliability:
			- Capability a product, to perform specified functions under specified conditions for a specified period of time.
				- Maturity.
				- Availability.
				- Fault tolerance.
				- Recoverability.
		- Maintainability:
			- Capability of a product to be modified by the intended maintainers with effectiveness and efficiency.
				- Modularity.
				- Reusability.
				- Analysability.
				- Modifiability.
				- Testability.
		- Flexibilit:
			- Capability of a product to serve a different or expanded set of requirements or contexts of use.
				- Adaptability.
				- Installability.
				- Replaceability.
		- Security:
			- Capability of a product to protect information and data so that persons or other products or systems have the degree of data access appropriate to their types and levels of authorization, and to defend against attack patterns by malicious actors.
				- Confidentiality.
				- Integrity.
				- Non-repudiation.
				- Accountability.
				- Authenticity.
		- Interaction capability:
			- Capability of a product to be used by specified users to achieve specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction in a specified context of use.
				- Recognizability.
				- Learnability.
				- Operability.
				- User error protection.
				- Interface.
				- Aesthetics.
				- Accessibility.
		- Performance efficiency:
			- Capability of a product to perform its functions within specified parameters and be efficient in the use of resources under stated condition.
				- Time behaviour.
				- Resource utilization.
				- Capacity.

Project Quality Management:
- The processes for incorporating the organization quality standards regarding:
	- Planning.
	- Managing.
	- Controlling Project and Product quality requirements to meet stakeholder’s goals.
- The quality of work is constrained by the project’s budget, deadlines and scope (features). 
	- The project manager can trade between constraints. Changes in one constraint necessitate changes in others to compensate or quality will suffer.
	- ![[Pasted image 20250525161119.png]]
- Key concepts:
	- Grade:
		- The characteristics of the product.
	- Quality:
		- How well something reflects the stated functionality.
	- Prevention:
		- Eliminating defects and potential defects from the process. 
		- This is the proactive approach to quality.
	- Inspection:
		- Fixing errors or defects as they come up in the process of creating the product or whatever is being tested or evaluated.
	- Cost of Quality:
		- Cost of conforming or not conforming to a continuous improvement approach to quality.
		- A company pays for quality one way or another:
			- Conformance to quality:
				- Immediate cost.
				- Positive effect.
			- Non-Conformance to quality:
				- Forward cost of reactive work (like rework).
				- Negative effect.
- Agile/Adaptive environments:
	- Due to increased requirements changes, adaptive life cycles require more frequent quality checks.
		- Each iteration has an explicit product review cycle to ensure quality control.
		- Each iteration has an explicit retrospective review to facilitate continuous process improvement.
- Major Components of Quality Management Plan: 
	- Quality Standards Used on Project.
	- Quality Objectives of Project.
	- Quality Roles and Responsibilities.
	- Project Deliverables and Processes that will be reviewed.
	- Quality Control and Quality Management Activities to be performed.
	- Quality Tools that will be used.
	- Major quality procedures.
